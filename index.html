Meridian

A war of aeroplanes, and the towns that build them. Pre-alpha demo.

Download for Windows
One file, about 110 MB. Double-click it and it unpacks and starts.
This link always gives you the newest build — all releases.

It is pre-alpha, which means exactly what it sounds like. Things will be missing, wrong, or plainly broken. That is what the demo is for, and a report from you is worth more than a polite silence.

What you are looking at

  • A town that genuinely simulates — farms, a mine, a mill, a bus with a driver who walks out to it.
  • An aerodrome, and pilots who fight over it. They see, plan, shoot and are frightened, and an ace does all four better.
  • Nothing is hidden: every person and machine can tell you what it is doing and why.

Keys

  • X follow a machine   Tab change machine   right-drag to look round
  • H the roll — who is flying, and how many they have shot down
  • F8 report a problem — takes a picture and gathers the rest for you
  • Esc the menu

If something goes wrong

Press F8 where it happened. It photographs what is on screen, asks what went wrong, and packages the picture, your note, the log and a census of the whole world into one file. That census is the useful part: it says where everything is, what everyone is doing, and how long since anything last moved.

Made by Aldmere. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
No account, no telemetry, nothing sent anywhere unless you press the button.
